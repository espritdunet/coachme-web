About CoachMe

Short description (~40 words). CoachMe is a 3.5 MB, fully offline Mac app for daily motivation. Schedule elegant floating cards — hand-picked quotes or messages you write to yourself — to appear at the right moment. No AI, no account, no tracking, no subscription.

Long description.

CoachMe turns daily motivation into a quiet ritual on your Mac. You create cards — either from a library of 700+ hand-curated quotes, or in your own words — and schedule them to appear as elegant floating windows: a morning push before work, an invitation to pause at noon, or a note you wrote to yourself a month ago, set to resurface today.

There's no AI generating anything, no account to create, no cloud sync, and no data leaving your machine. Everything is stored locally. The app weighs 3.5 MB and runs natively on Apple Silicon.

It's free to use with up to 3 cards. A single one-time purchase (CoachMe Pro) unlocks unlimited cards and surprise cards drawn from the full library — no subscription, ever. CoachMe is built and maintained by one person, and the quote library keeps growing with every update.

Key features

  • Your words or ours — write personal goals, affirmations, and reminders, or draw from 700+ curated quotes across philosophy, leadership, stoicism, and mindfulness (722 quotes by 93 authors today, growing with each update).
  • Smart scheduling — by time of day, recurring interval, or specific weekdays. Cards appear as elegant floating windows, then disappear. Pause everything during focus blocks, evenings, or weekends.
  • Surprise cards (Pro) — with CoachMe Pro, let the app draw a card from the full library automatically through your day, an unobtrusive invitation to pause and breathe.
  • Beautiful by design — 10 curated gradients or your own palette, 24 fonts, icons, and any photo from your library as a card background.
  • Built for your language — full support for English, French, German, and Spanish: app, interface, and quote library.
  • Privacy by design — all data stored locally via SwiftData inside the app sandbox; sensitive content redacted from system logs; automatic local backups before major updates.

What makes it different

In a market full of "AI-powered", always-connected, subscription-based motivation apps, CoachMe is the deliberate opposite — software the way it used to be.

  • No AI. Every quote is hand-curated and verified against its source. Nothing is generated.
  • 100% offline. No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics. The developer literally cannot know when you open the app.
  • Pay once. A single purchase, owned forever. No subscription, no renewal.
  • Featherlight. 3.5 MB — yes, megabytes.
  • Solo-built. Made and maintained by one independent developer, not a venture-funded attention machine.
  • Honest in scope. It won't "transform your life". It offers a small, well-crafted ritual — and does it beautifully.
