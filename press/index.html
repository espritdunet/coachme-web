About CoachMe

Short description (~40 words). CoachMe is a featherlight app for daily motivation on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Schedule cards — hand-picked quotes or messages you write to yourself — to appear at the right moment, kept in sync by iCloud. No AI, no account, no tracking, no subscription.

Long description.

CoachMe turns daily motivation into a quiet ritual. You create cards — either from a library of 700+ hand-curated quotes, or in your own words — and schedule them to reach you at the right moment: a morning push before work, an invitation to pause at noon, or a note you wrote to yourself a month ago, set to resurface today. On iPhone and iPad, cards arrive as a notification, on a widget and in the app; on the Mac, they float on your desktop.

There's no AI generating anything, no account to create, and no server of ours in the loop. Cards, photos and settings live on your devices and, if you wish, travel between them through your own private iCloud — which the developer cannot read. The app weighs just a few megabytes.

It's free to use with up to 3 cards. A single one-time purchase (CoachMe Pro) unlocks unlimited cards and surprise cards drawn from the full library — no subscription, ever. Pro follows your Apple Account on all your devices and can be shared through Family Sharing. CoachMe is built and maintained by one person, and the quote library keeps growing with every update.

New in version 2.0 (October 2026)

  • Now on iPhone and iPad — CoachMe was Mac-only. Version 2.0 brings it to iPhone and iPad, where cards arrive as a notification, on a home-screen widget and in the app.
  • Private iCloud sync — cards, photos and settings stay in sync across devices through the user's own private iCloud database (CloudKit). Optional, and invisible to the developer.
  • One purchase, every device — CoachMe Pro follows the user's Apple Account on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and supports Family Sharing. Still a one-time purchase.
  • A quieter look — black and white by default, with the user's own photo as a backdrop. Holi, the free color mode, stays one tap away (and stays on for existing users who already used color).
  • Wider Mac support — now runs on macOS 15 or later.

Key features

  • Your words or ours — write personal goals, affirmations, and reminders, or draw from 700+ curated quotes across philosophy, leadership, stoicism, and mindfulness (growing with each update).
  • Smart scheduling — by time of day, recurring interval, or specific weekdays. On the Mac, cards appear as elegant floating windows, then disappear; on iPhone and iPad, as a notification and on a widget. Pause everything during focus blocks, evenings, or weekends.
  • Surprise cards — let the app draw a card for you through your day, an unobtrusive invitation to pause and breathe. Free from your own cards; with CoachMe Pro, from the full quote library, your cards, or both.
  • Beautiful by design — understated black and white by default, any photo from your library as a card background, or Holi, the free color mode.
  • Everywhere you are — iPhone, iPad and Mac, kept in sync through your own private iCloud.
  • Built for your language — full support for English, French, German, and Spanish: app, interface, and quote library.
  • Privacy by design — data stored on-device via SwiftData inside the app sandbox, optionally synced through the user's private iCloud; sensitive content redacted from system logs; automatic local backups before major updates.

What makes it different

In a market full of "AI-powered", always-connected, subscription-based motivation apps, CoachMe is the deliberate opposite — software the way it used to be.

  • No AI. Every quote is hand-curated and verified against its source. Nothing is generated.
  • No servers, no tracking. No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics. Sync runs through your own private iCloud. The developer literally cannot know when you open the app.
  • Pay once, use everywhere. A single purchase, owned forever on iPhone, iPad and Mac, shareable with family. No subscription, no renewal.
  • Featherlight. Just a few megabytes — yes, megabytes.
  • Solo-built. Made and maintained by one independent developer, not a venture-funded attention machine.
  • Honest in scope. It won't "transform your life". It offers a small, well-crafted ritual — and does it beautifully.
