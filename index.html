Privacy Policy

"It's very light, clean, respectful of my environment — and I love how the reminder shows up at just the right moment, with a personal message you wrote to yourself." — Early user

Effective Date: February 14, 2026
CoachMe is built on the principle of Privacy by Design. Your inspiration is your business, not ours.

🔒 100% Offline & Private

CoachMe runs entirely locally. No accounts, no telemetry, and no hidden analytics (like Firebase or Google Analytics). We don't even know when you open the app.

💾 Local Storage

Everything is stored on your Mac using SwiftData and UserDefaults. Your custom images remain within the App's secure sandbox.

🛠 Technical Transparency

We use Apple's Unified Logging (OSLog) for diagnostics. All sensitive content (your card text and file paths) is explicitly marked as <private>. This means it is automatically redacted and never visible in system logs unless you are debugging the app yourself.

🔄 Local Backups

Automatic backups are created before major updates, but they never leave your machine. They are stored in your Application Support folder and can be managed directly from the app settings.

🌐 Third-Party Services

The app does not communicate with any external servers. The only third-party interaction is with standard macOS services (like iCloud if you manually choose to sync your files).

Since we collect no data, we do not track or profile users, including children under 13. For any privacy-related questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you create up to 3 custom cards — with your own messages or quotes drawn from the built-in library. Pro (€9.99 one-time) unlocks two things:

  • Unlimited custom cards
  • Surprise cards drawn automatically from the full library throughout your day

That's it. All customization options — gradients, photos, fonts, scheduling — are fully unlocked from the start, in both Free and Pro. No artificial limits.

Is the €9.99 really a one-time purchase?

Yes — pay once, own it forever. No subscription, no recurring charge, no renewal. Future quote-library updates and new languages are included at no extra cost.

Which macOS versions are supported?

CoachMe requires macOS 26 Tahoe or later and runs natively on Apple Silicon (M1, M2, M3, M4) for the best performance and battery efficiency.

How can I customize my cards?

Deeply. You can:

  • Write your own messages — goals, mantras, reminders, words to yourself
  • Choose from 10 curated gradient backgrounds, or build your own color palette
  • Use any photo from your library as a card background — your dreams, places, loved ones
  • Pick fonts, sizes, icons — every visual detail

Your cards stay yours, even when the quote comes from the library.

Can I control when cards appear?

Absolutely — you set the rhythm. Schedule specific times, regular intervals during work hours, or let surprise cards land throughout the day. Pause everything during focus blocks, evenings, weekends — whenever you need silence.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your Mac. CoachMe uses local SwiftData and UserDefaults — no cloud, no account, no telemetry. We don't even know when you open the app. Local backups are saved before major updates so you never lose your cards.

Will new quotes and languages keep coming?

Yes — the library is the core ongoing work. The goal is to grow toward several thousand quotes and add more languages over time. Your Pro purchase funds this directly, and all updates are free for Pro users.
