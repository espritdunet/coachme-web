Privacy Policy

"It's very light, clean, respectful of my environment — and I love how the reminder shows up at just the right moment, with a personal message you wrote to yourself." — Early user

Effective Date: October 15, 2026
CoachMe is built on the principle of Privacy by Design. Your inspiration is your business, not ours.

🔒 No account, no tracking

CoachMe has no account system, no telemetry and no hidden analytics (like Firebase or Google Analytics). We don't even know when you open the app.

☁️ Your cards, your iCloud

Your cards, photos and settings are stored on your device using SwiftData and UserDefaults. If iCloud is enabled for CoachMe, they are synced through your own private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit).

🍏 What iCloud sync means

Synced data is tied to your Apple Account and handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. As the developer, I have no access to it: it never passes through any server of ours. Sync is optional — you can turn it off at any time in your device's iCloud settings, and CoachMe keeps working locally.

🛠 Technical Transparency

We use Apple's Unified Logging (OSLog) for diagnostics. All sensitive content (your card text and file paths) is explicitly marked as <private>. This means it is automatically redacted and never visible in system logs unless you are debugging the app yourself.

🔄 Local Backups

Automatic backups are created before major updates. They are stored in the app's private space on your device and are never sent anywhere.

💳 Purchases

CoachMe Pro is bought through the App Store. Payment and Family Sharing are handled entirely by Apple — we never see your payment details or your identity.

🌐 Third-Party Services

CoachMe has no servers of its own and uses no third-party services other than Apple's: iCloud (for sync, if enabled) and the App Store (for purchases).

Since we collect no data, we do not track or profile users, including children under 13. For any privacy-related questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.

Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?

Free lets you create up to 3 custom cards, and Surprise mode draws from those cards. Pro (€9.99 one-time) unlocks two things:

  • Unlimited custom cards
  • Surprise cards drawn from the full quote library, from your own cards, or from both

That's it. All customization options — black and white or Holi color mode, your own photos, scheduling — are unlocked from the start, in both Free and Pro. No artificial limits.

Is the €9.99 really a one-time purchase?

Yes — pay once, own it forever, on all your devices. No subscription, no recurring charge, no renewal. Future quote-library updates and new languages are included at no extra cost.

Does CoachMe work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, since version 2.0. On iPhone and iPad, your cards arrive as a notification, on a widget and in the app. On the Mac, they float on your desktop. iCloud keeps your cards, photos and settings in sync between all your devices.

Do I have to buy Pro again on each device?

No. CoachMe Pro is tied to your Apple Account: buy it once on any device and it's unlocked on your iPhone, iPad and Mac. With Family Sharing, the members of your family get it too.

Which devices are supported?

CoachMe runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It requires iOS 18.6 or later on iPhone, iPadOS 18.6 or later on iPad, and macOS 15 or later on the Mac.

How can I customize my cards?

Your way. You can:

  • Write your own messages — goals, mantras, reminders, words to yourself
  • Keep the understated black and white style, or turn on Holi, the free color mode
  • Use any of your photos as a card background — your dreams, places, loved ones

Your cards stay yours, even when the quote comes from the library.

Can I control when cards appear?

Absolutely — you set the rhythm. Schedule specific times, regular intervals during work hours, or let surprise cards land throughout the day. Pause everything during focus blocks, evenings, weekends — whenever you need silence.

Where is my data stored?

On your devices, and in your own private iCloud if iCloud is enabled for CoachMe. Never on our servers — we don't have any. No account, no telemetry: we don't even know when you open the app. You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in your device's settings.

I'm upgrading from CoachMe 1.x. Will I lose my cards?

No. CoachMe makes a backup before it changes anything. If you already use color, Holi, the color mode, stays on and your cards look just as before. You can switch to black and white anytime in Settings.

Will new quotes and languages keep coming?

Yes — the library is the core ongoing work. The goal is to grow toward several thousand quotes and add more languages over time. Your Pro purchase funds this directly, and all updates are free for Pro users.
